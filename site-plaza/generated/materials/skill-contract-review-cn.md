# contract-review-cn

## What this is

中国法合同审查。判定合同适用中国大陆法后，加载通用原则与五张行业审查卡（劳动/借贷/保密/买卖/SaaS），逐条审查并产出双轴风险问题清单与审查备忘录；涉精确法条、阈值、生效日期时经法律数据工具核验。当用户要求审查/审阅/markup 合同、检查条款、问某条款在中国法下是否有效或如何修改时使用。仅覆盖中国大陆法，不含港澳台、不含刑事与行政、不含外国法规则包。

Contract review under Chinese law. After determining that the contract is governed by mainland China law, loads general principles plus five industry review cards (labor/loans/confidentiality/sales/SaaS), reviews clause by clause, and produces a dual-axis risk issue list and a review memo; precise statutes, thresholds, and effective dates are verified through legal data tools. Use when the user asks to review/annotate/mark up a contract, check clauses, or asks whether a clause is valid under Chinese law or how to amend it. Covers mainland China law only — no Hong Kong/Macau/Taiwan, no criminal or administrative law, no foreign-law rule packs.
