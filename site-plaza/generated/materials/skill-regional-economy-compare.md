# regional-economy-compare

## What this is

区域经济比较：对中国城市/省份做多维指标横向排名、分位定位、增长结构分解与相似城市聚类。当用户要比较两个或多个城市/省份的经济实力、产业结构、财政与居民收入，要某城市在全省或全国的排名与分位，要拆解经济总量增长的产业贡献与拉动点数，或要按结构找与目标城市可比的对照组时使用。数据走柏讯城市面板与统计年鉴。

Regional economic comparison: multi-dimensional indicator rankings, percentile positioning, growth-structure decomposition, and similar-city clustering for Chinese cities/provinces. Use when the user wants to compare the economic strength, industrial structure, fiscal capacity, and household income of two or more cities/provinces; wants a city's rank and percentile within its province or nationwide; wants the industrial contributions and pull points behind total growth decomposed; or wants a structurally comparable control group for a target city. Data comes from 柏讯 city panels and statistical yearbooks.
