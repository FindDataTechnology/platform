# advertising-compliance-review

## What this is

广告合规审核技能，用于审核广告素材是否符合中国广告法及相关法规。适用场景：(1) 用户提交广告文案、广告素材要求合规审核时；(2) 用户提到"广告审核""广告合规""广告法审查"等关键词时；(3) 用户要求检查广告内容是否存在违法违规风险时；(4) 用户提交房地产、食品、医疗、药品、互联网等行业广告要求专项审核时。审核依据涵盖《广告法》《反不正当竞争法》及行业专项法规。

Advertising compliance review skill for checking whether advertising materials comply with China's Advertising Law and related regulations. Applicable scenarios: (1) the user submits ad copy or ad materials requesting compliance review; (2) the user mentions keywords such as "ad review", "ad compliance", or "Advertising Law review"; (3) the user asks to check whether ad content carries legal or regulatory violation risks; (4) the user submits ads from industries such as real estate, food, healthcare, pharmaceuticals, or the internet for specialized review. The review basis covers the Advertising Law, the Anti-Unfair Competition Law, and industry-specific regulations.
