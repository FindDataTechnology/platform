# mb-margin-structure-calculator

## What this is

毛利结构计算器（谦面·个体户）：固定/变动成本拆分、各项成本占流水比、毛利率与边际贡献率、成本结构诊断。每数字附公式+基数+来源，拆分结果供盈亏平衡计算器消费。

Gross margin structure calculator (Facet · sole proprietors): fixed/variable cost split, each cost as a share of revenue, gross margin and contribution margin, and cost-structure diagnostics. Every figure comes with formula + base + source; the split results feed the break-even calculator.

- Species: skill (plaza category enum v1)
- Official: no
