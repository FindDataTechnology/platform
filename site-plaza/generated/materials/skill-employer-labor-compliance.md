# employer-labor-compliance

## What this is

公司端劳动用工合规 Skill。用于用人单位日常劳动合规、员工手册/规章制度/劳动合同更新、工资工时问答、内部劳动政策起草，以及公司在解除、调岗、降薪、处罚、员工投诉或仲裁前的风险预判。用户提到公司劳动合规、员工手册、规章制度、薪酬制度、考勤制度、加班费、发薪日、特殊工时、劳动合同修改、内部政策起草、辞退员工风险、员工投诉公司、调岗降薪等场景时触发。

Company-side labor and employment compliance skill. Used for the employer's day-to-day labor compliance, updates to employee handbooks/internal rules/labor contracts, wage and working-hours Q&A, drafting internal labor policies, and pre-emptive risk assessment before the company dismisses, transfers, cuts pay, disciplines, receives employee complaints, or faces arbitration. Triggered when the user mentions company labor compliance, employee handbooks, internal rules, compensation systems, attendance systems, overtime pay, paydays, special working hours, labor contract amendments, internal policy drafting, dismissal risk, employee complaints against the company, or transfers and pay cuts.
