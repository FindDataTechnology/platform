# legal-wiki-lookup

## What this is

按罪名/领域/关键词检索 Wiki 知识页，返回知识卡片或全文内容。 支持刑事罪名知识、民事领域知识、案例知识页的查询与交叉引用跳转。 当用户提及"查Wiki"、"知识库查询"、"查罪名"、"查法律知识"、"wiki查询"等关键词时触发。

Searches Wiki knowledge pages by crime/charge, practice area, or keyword, returning knowledge cards or full-text content. Supports queries and cross-reference jumps across criminal charge knowledge, civil area knowledge, and case knowledge pages. Triggers when the user mentions keywords such as "check the Wiki", "knowledge base query", "look up a charge", "look up legal knowledge", or "wiki query".

- Species: skill (plaza category enum v1)
- Official: no



## How to obtain

1. In 壹座 (Yizuo): Extensions → Skills — subscribe from the skills face.
2. Inside editors: skills reach your editor bundled with a pack that references them — install that pack with:

       npx @finddatatechnology/facet install <packId>

3. Deployed agent services receive registry skills through the registry supply channel.
