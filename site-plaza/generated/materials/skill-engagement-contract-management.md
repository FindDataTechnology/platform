# engagement-contract-management

## What this is

协助律师完成委托合同签订和案件归档。签订阶段覆盖收费方式确认、授权范围确认、全套委托文书生成；归档阶段覆盖诉讼跟踪记录表生成和客户档案更新。当用户发送客户编号并提及"签订合同"、"案件建档"等指令时触发使用。

Assists lawyers with signing engagement contracts and archiving case files. The signing stage covers confirmation of the fee arrangement, confirmation of the scope of authorization, and generation of the full set of engagement documents; the archiving stage covers generation of litigation tracking record sheets and updating client files. Triggers when the user sends a client number and mentions instructions such as "sign the contract" or "create the case file".
