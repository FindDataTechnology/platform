# labor-arbitration-case-management

## What this is

劳动争议仲裁程序全流程管理Skill，覆盖仲裁前置强制检测、仲裁时效计算、一裁终局自动判断、仲裁申请书/答辩书生成（W/E双角色）、仲裁请求清单、仲裁→诉讼衔接路由（L9五分支）、仲裁策略报告和用人单位仲裁前风险敞口评估。当用户发送客户编号并提及"仲裁申请"、"仲裁答辩"、"仲裁策略"、"仲裁时效"、"公司仲裁风险"、"风险敞口"等指令时触发使用。

Full-workflow management skill for labor dispute arbitration, covering mandatory arbitration-prerequisite detection, arbitration limitation-period calculation, automatic one-award-finality determination, generation of arbitration applications/responses (dual W/E roles), arbitration claim checklists, arbitration-to-litigation routing (L9 five branches), arbitration strategy reports, and pre-arbitration risk-exposure assessment for employers. Triggers when the user sends a client number and mentions instructions such as "arbitration application", "arbitration response", "arbitration strategy", "arbitration limitation period", "company arbitration risk", or "risk exposure".
