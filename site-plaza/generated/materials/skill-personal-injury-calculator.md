# personal-injury-calculator

## What this is

人身损害赔偿计算器（识律劳动法套件原子技能，双计算器之一）。当用户要求计算人身损害赔偿项目时使用——医疗费、误工费、护理费、交通费、住院伙食补助费、营养费、残疾赔偿金、残疾辅助器具费、丧葬费、死亡赔偿金、被扶养人生活费、精神损害抚慰金。法律依据走识律条级解析，残疾/死亡赔偿金等基数走柏讯统计源，每个结果强制附公式与基数来源。

Personal injury damages calculator (atomic skill of the 识律 labor law suite, one of the two calculators). Use when the user asks to compute personal injury compensation items — medical expenses, lost earnings, nursing fees, transportation costs, hospital meal allowances, nutrition fees, disability compensation, disability assistive device costs, funeral expenses, death compensation, dependents' living expenses, and mental distress damages. Legal bases come from 识律 article-level parsing; bases for disability/death compensation etc. come from the 柏讯 statistics source; every result must carry its formula and base source.
