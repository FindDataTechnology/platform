# case-intake-and-engagement

## What this is

刑事辩护案件承接与委托Skill。协助律师完成刑事案件的初次接待、案件评估、风险告知、委托手续办理及案件信息采集，生成标准化委托文书并建立案件档案。当用户发送客户编号并提及"刑事案件承接"、"刑事委托"、"刑事接案"等指令时触发使用。

Skill for taking on and engaging criminal defense cases. Assists lawyers with initial reception, case assessment, risk disclosure, engagement formalities, and case information collection for criminal cases; generates standardized engagement documents and establishes case files. Triggers when the user sends a client number and mentions instructions such as "take on a criminal case", "criminal engagement", or "criminal case intake".

- Species: skill (plaza category enum v1)
- Official: no

1. In 壹座 (Yizuo): Extensions → Skills — subscribe from the skills face.
