# industry-chain-analysis

## What this is

行业景气与产业链传导分析。当用户要判断一个行业处在景气周期的哪个位置、梳理上下游投入产出与成本价格传导路径、用上市公司财务面板实证行业判断、刻画行业格局（规模/增速/集中度/进入壁垒），或把研报观点与公司真实财务数据对照时使用。走柏讯服务端行业与公司财务数据，不涉及价格与市值分析。

Industry climate and value-chain transmission analysis. Use when the user wants to determine where an industry sits in the business cycle, map upstream/downstream input-output and cost-price transmission paths, substantiate industry judgments with listed-company financial panels, characterize the industry landscape (size/growth/concentration/entry barriers), or check research-report views against companies' actual financial data. Uses 柏讯 server-side industry and company financial data; does not cover price or market-cap analysis.
