# evidence-evaluation

## What this is

当需要对案件中的证据材料进行真实性、合法性、关联性（证据"三性"）及证明力评估时触发此技能。 典型触发场景包括但不限于： - 当事人或代理人提交证据后，需要判断该证据能否被法庭采纳 - 对方当事人对己方证据提出质证意见，需要进行回应和分析 - 需要对全案证据进行综合评判，确定待证事实是否成立 - 需要判断证据是否达到相应的证明标准（如高度盖然性、排除合理怀疑等） - 需要识别证据链中的薄弱环节并提出补强建议 - 电子数据、鉴定意见、证人证言等特殊证据形式的效力判断 - 非法证据排除的识别与论证

Triggers when the case's evidence materials need assessment of authenticity, legality, and relevance (the "three properties" of evidence) and of probative force. Typical trigger scenarios include but are not limited to: after a party or agent submits evidence, judging whether the court can admit it; responding to and analyzing the opposing party's cross-examination objections to one's own evidence; comprehensively evaluating all the case's evidence to determine whether the facts to be proven stand; judging whether the evidence meets the applicable proof standard (e.g., high preponderance, exclusion of reasonable doubt); identifying weak links in the evidence chain and proposing corroboration suggestions; determining the validity of special evidence forms such as electronic data, expert opinions, and witness testimony; recognizing and arguing the exclusion of illegally obtained evidence.
