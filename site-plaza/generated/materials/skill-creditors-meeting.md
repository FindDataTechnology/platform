# creditors-meeting

## What this is

系统化支持债权人会议的筹备、召开、表决、纪要全流程工作，覆盖管理人、债权人代理、债务人代理三角色。支持第一次及后续多次债权人会议。当用户发送客户编号并提及"债权人会议"、"召开会议"、"准备会议"、"会议通知"、"会议议程"、"会议纪要"等指令时触发使用。

Systematically supports the full workflow of creditors' meetings — preparation, convening, voting, and minutes — covering three roles: administrator, creditors' agent, and debtor's agent. Supports the first and subsequent multiple creditors' meetings. Triggered when the user sends a client number and mentions instructions such as "creditors' meeting", "convene a meeting", "prepare a meeting", "meeting notice", "meeting agenda", or "meeting minutes".

- Species: skill (plaza category enum v1)
- Official: no
