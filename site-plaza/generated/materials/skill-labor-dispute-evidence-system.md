# labor-dispute-evidence-system

## What this is

劳动争议案件证据管理专属Skill，覆盖举证责任倒置规则、按12类争议类型自动生成证据收集清单、证据效力三级评估、四类特殊保护自动检测、规章制度合法性审查三要件、劳动者代理证据完整性检查和用人单位争议/合规证据清单。输出举证责任分配表、证据收集清单、特殊保护检测报告、公司端事实与制度证据缺口表。当用户发送客户编号并提及"举证责任"、"劳动争议证据"、"证据清单"、"特殊保护"、"员工手册证据"、"公司端证据"等指令时触发使用。

Dedicated evidence-management skill for labor dispute cases, covering burden-of-proof reversal rules, automatic generation of evidence collection checklists by 12 dispute types, three-tier assessment of evidentiary weight, automatic detection of four types of special protection, the three elements of legality review of internal rules, evidence completeness checks for worker representation, and employer dispute/compliance evidence checklists. Outputs a burden-of-proof allocation table, evidence collection checklists, special-protection detection reports, and company-side fact-and-policy evidence gap tables. Triggers when the user sends a client number and mentions instructions such as "burden of proof", "labor dispute evidence", "evidence checklist", "special protection", "employee handbook evidence", or "company-side evidence".
