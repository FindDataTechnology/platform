# formal-legal-consequence

## What this is

当需要根据已确认的法律事实、已识别的法律关系和已匹配的法律规范，推导出具体的法律后果（包括但不限于民事责任类型与范围、损害赔偿金额计算、刑事刑期确定、行政处罚种类与幅度、合同效力状态等）时，触发本技能。 典型触发场景包括： - 用户询问"我能获得多少赔偿""对方要承担什么责任" - 用户询问"这个行为会判几年""罚款多少" - 需要从法律构成要件满足情况推导出具体法律效果 - 需要计算具体赔偿数额、刑期范围、违约金数额等量化结果 - 需要综合考虑加重、减轻、免除等调整因素后得出最终法律后果 本技能是法律推理链条的终端环节，将抽象的法律规范转化为具体的、可操作的法律结论。

Triggers when specific legal consequences must be derived from confirmed legal facts, identified legal relations, and matched legal norms — including but not limited to the type and scope of civil liability, damages calculation, criminal sentence determination, administrative penalty types and ranges, and contract validity status. Typical trigger scenarios include: the user asks "how much compensation can I get" or "what liability will the other party bear"; asks "how many years will this act draw" or "how big is the fine"; deriving concrete legal effects from the satisfaction of legal constitutive elements; computing quantified results such as compensation amounts, sentence ranges, and liquidated damages amounts; weighing aggravating, mitigating, and exempting factors to reach the final legal consequence. This skill is the terminal link of the legal reasoning chain, converting abstract legal norms into concrete, actionable legal conclusions.
