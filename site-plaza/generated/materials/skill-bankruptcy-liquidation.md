# bankruptcy-liquidation

## What this is

覆盖破产清算程序全流程，从宣告破产到程序终结。主要为R1（管理人）工作，R2/R3参与监督。当用户发送客户编号并提及"破产清算"、"财产变价"、"财产分配"、"终结程序"等指令时触发使用。

Covers the full bankruptcy liquidation procedure, from the declaration of bankruptcy to the closure of the proceeding. Primarily work for R1 (the administrator), with R2/R3 participating in oversight. Triggered when the user sends a client number and mentions commands such as 'bankruptcy liquidation', 'asset monetization', 'asset distribution', or 'close the proceeding'.
