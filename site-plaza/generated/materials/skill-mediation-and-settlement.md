# mediation-and-settlement

## What this is

提供调解与和解全流程支持，包括可行性分析、调解方案起草、调解书/和解协议审查、撤诉管理等功能。当用户发送客户编号并提及"调解"、"和解"、"撤诉"、"调解方案"、"调解书"等指令时触发使用。

Provides full-process support for mediation and settlement, including feasibility analysis, drafting mediation proposals, review of mediation statements/settlement agreements, and withdrawal-of-claim management. Triggered when the user sends a client number and mentions commands such as 'mediation', 'settlement', 'withdraw the claim', 'mediation proposal', or 'mediation statement'.

- Species: skill (plaza category enum v1)
- Official: no
