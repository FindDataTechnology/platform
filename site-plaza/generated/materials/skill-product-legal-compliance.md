Product legal and compliance skill for the Chinese market. Used for product launches, feature compliance, default-on settings, customer logo/case-study display, review of PRDs/page flows/user agreements/privacy policies/help centers, compliance review of product materials, in-depth assessment of products in high-compliance industries, and initial screening of product scenarios in marketing copy. Suited to questions such as "can this feature be built", "can we put a customer's logo on the official website", "are the product materials lawful and compliant", and "can a healthcare/finance/education/AI/platform product go live".
