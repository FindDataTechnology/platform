# civil-judgment-drafting

## What this is

一审普通程序民事判决书生成工具。从已有案件材料（起诉状、答辩状、庭审纪要、法律分析等）提取要素，生成符合法院文书样式标准的Word判决书初稿。按总控当前事项记录和复盘台账衔接。当用户发送客户编号并提及"判决书"、"民事判决书"、"起草判决书"、"模拟判决书"等指令时触发使用。

A first-instance ordinary-procedure civil judgment drafting tool. Extracts elements from existing case materials (complaints, defenses, hearing minutes, legal analyses, etc.) to generate a draft Word judgment that conforms to court document style standards. Links to the master controller's current-matter records and retrospective ledger. Triggers when the user sends a client number and mentions instructions such as "judgment", "civil judgment", "draft a judgment", or "simulated judgment".
