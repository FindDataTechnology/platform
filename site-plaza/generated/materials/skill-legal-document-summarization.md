# legal-document-summarization

## What this is

当需要对判决书、裁定书、调解书、仲裁裁决书、行政处罚决定书等法律文书进行结构化摘要时触发此技能。 典型触发场景包括但不限于： - 用户要求对一份法律文书做摘要、提炼要点或生成裁判要旨 - 用户需要将多份法律文书转化为可对比、可检索的标准化摘要 - 用户要求提取案件信息、争议焦点、法律依据或说理过程 - 用户需要为案例研究、诉讼分析、教学备课准备摘要材料 - 用户要求区分不同文书类型（判决/裁定/调解/仲裁/行政处罚）的摘要重点 本技能的核心价值：在忠实于原文的基础上，将冗长法律文书转化为简明、客观、结构化的摘要，突出核心内容，避免复述全文和主观评价。

Triggers when structured summaries are needed for legal documents such as judgments, rulings, mediation agreements, arbitration awards, and administrative penalty decisions. Typical trigger scenarios include but are not limited to: the user asks to summarize a legal document, distill key points, or generate the gist of the ruling; needs to convert multiple legal documents into comparable, searchable standardized summaries; asks to extract case information, disputed issues, legal bases, or reasoning processes; needs summary material for case studies, litigation analysis, or teaching preparation; asks to differentiate summary emphases by document type (judgment/ruling/mediation/arbitration/administrative penalty). Core value of this skill: faithfully converting lengthy legal documents into concise, objective, structured summaries that highlight the core content, avoiding restating the full text and subjective commentary.
