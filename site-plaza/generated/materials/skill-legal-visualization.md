# legal-visualization

## What this is

Legal Visualization。面向法律业务场景的法律图解与图表生成技能；当用户要求把案件材料、合同材料、合规事项、交易安排、证据链、诉讼流程、时间轴、法律关系、客户汇报、法律服务方案或律师团队工作整理成关系图、流程图、时间轴、证据链、风险图、路线图、PNG/SVG/PDF/.drawio 时使用；也兼容“法律可视化”“案件事实图”“法律关系图”等说法。先按受众、任务动词和路由规则筛选场景，再生成可交付图片，并保留 draw.io 源文件作为可编辑底稿。本技能不用于事实核验，也不替代法律结论判断。

Legal Visualization. A skill for legal diagrams and chart generation in legal business scenarios; use when the user asks to organize case materials, contract materials, compliance matters, transaction arrangements, evidence chains, litigation processes, timelines, legal relationships, client reports, legal service proposals, or lawyer team work into relationship diagrams, flowcharts, timelines, evidence chains, risk maps, roadmaps, or PNG/SVG/PDF/.drawio outputs; also compatible with phrasings like 'legal visualization', 'case fact diagram', or 'legal relationship diagram'. Scenarios are first screened by audience, task verbs, and routing rules, then deliverable images are generated, with draw.io source files retained as editable drafts. This skill is not for fact verification and does not substitute for legal conclusion judgment.
