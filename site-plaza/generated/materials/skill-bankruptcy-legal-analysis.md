# bankruptcy-legal-analysis

## What this is

基于《企业破产法》及司法解释，进行破产案件的系统性法律分析，支持管理人、债权人代理、债务人代理三种角色视角，生成标准化破产法律分析报告Word文档。当用户发送客户编号并提及"破产法律分析"、"破产分析"指令时触发使用，或在破产案件流程中自动衔接（BP1→BP2）。

Performs systematic legal analysis of bankruptcy cases based on the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law and its judicial interpretations, supporting three role perspectives - administrator, creditor's counsel, and debtor's counsel - and generating a standardized bankruptcy legal analysis report as a Word document. Triggered when the user sends a client number and mentions the commands 'bankruptcy legal analysis' or 'bankruptcy analysis', or automatically chained within the bankruptcy case flow (BP1→BP2).
