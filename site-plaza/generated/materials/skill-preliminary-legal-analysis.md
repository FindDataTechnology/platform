# preliminary-legal-analysis

## What this is

基于"六来源体系"、请求权基础6步法与要件审判九步法融合框架，进行系统性法律分析，按总控当前事项记录和复盘台账衔接，生成法律服务建议书Word文档。当用户发送客户编号并提及"初步法律分析"、"法律分析"、"请求权分析"等指令时触发使用。

Performs systematic legal analysis based on the fused framework of the "six-source system", the 6-step claim-basis method, and the nine-step element-based adjudication method; links to the master controller's current-matter records and retrospective ledger to generate a legal service proposal as a Word document. Triggers when the user sends a client number and mentions instructions such as "preliminary legal analysis", "legal analysis", or "claim analysis".
