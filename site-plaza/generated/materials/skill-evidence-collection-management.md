# evidence-collection-management

## What this is

基于请求权要件分析进行证据缺口评估，系统化指引证据收集、调查取证、证人管理和财产保全全流程，生成取证方案、各类申请文书和调查笔录。当用户发送客户编号并提及"调查取证"、"证据收集"、"财产保全"、"调查令"、"证人出庭"、"补充材料"、"取证方案"等指令时触发使用。

Assesses evidence gaps based on claim-element analysis and systematically guides the full process of evidence collection, investigative evidence-gathering, witness management, and asset preservation, producing evidence plans, various application documents, and investigation transcripts. Triggered when the user sends a client number and mentions commands such as 'investigation and evidence gathering', 'evidence collection', 'asset preservation', 'investigation order', 'witness appearance', 'supplementary materials', or 'evidence plan'.
