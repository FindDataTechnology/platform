# civil-first-instance-litigation

## What this is

民事一审诉讼与破产案件专业流程编排 Skill。整合25个子Skill：民事诉讼14个（法律咨询助手、案件沟通记录、初步法律分析、委托合同管理、法规案例检索、调查取证与证据管理、立案管理、诉讼可视化、案件讨论与提纲、诉讼文书起草、庭前准备、庭审与庭后工作、调解与和解、诉讼分析工具、结案归档）+ 破产案件10个（破产法律分析、破产申请与受理、管理人工作、财产调查与管理、债权申报与审查、债权人会议、重整程序、破产和解程序、破产清算、破产文书生成）。用于判断民事/破产案件阶段（S0-S16/SM/BP系列）、推荐下一步子Skill、提示关键节点确认和期限；事项建档、路径、文件读取、来源披露、OCR 校正和缺口归档由「法律工作总控」统一处理。

Professional workflow-orchestration skill for civil first-instance litigation and bankruptcy cases. Integrates 25 sub-skills: 14 civil litigation skills (legal consultation assistant, case communication records, preliminary legal analysis, engagement contract management, statute and case retrieval, investigation and evidence collection and evidence management, case filing management, litigation visualization, case discussion and outline, litigation document drafting, pretrial preparation, trial and post-trial work, mediation and settlement, litigation analysis tools, and case closing and archiving) plus 10 bankruptcy skills (bankruptcy legal analysis, bankruptcy application and acceptance, administrator work, asset investigation and management, creditor claim registration and review, creditors' meetings, reorganization procedures, bankruptcy settlement procedures, bankruptcy liquidation, and bankruptcy document generation). Used to determine the civil/bankruptcy case stage (S0-S16/SM/BP series), recommend the next sub-skill, and flag key-node confirmations and deadlines; matter filing, paths, file reading, source disclosure, OCR correction, and gap archiving are handled uniformly by the "legal work master controller".
