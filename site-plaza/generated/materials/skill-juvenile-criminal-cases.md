# juvenile-criminal-cases

## What this is

未成年人刑事案件专项辩护Skill，覆盖未成年人刑事案件的教育感化挽救原则、社会调查、不逮捕申请、附条件不起诉、犯罪记录封存、不公开审理、法定代理人到场和信息保护等工作。当用户提及"未成年人"、"少年犯"、"社会调查"、"附条件不起诉"、"犯罪记录封存"、"不公开审理"等关键词时触发使用。

Specialized defense skill for juvenile criminal cases, covering the education-influence-rescue principle for juvenile criminal cases, social investigation, non-arrest applications, conditional non-prosecution, criminal record sealing, non-public trials, presence of legal representatives, and information protection. Triggers when the user mentions keywords such as "juvenile", "juvenile offender", "social investigation", "conditional non-prosecution", "criminal record sealing", or "non-public trial".
