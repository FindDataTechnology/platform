# internal-compliance-risk-identification

## What this is

当需要对企业内部的合规管理体系进行系统性审查，识别制度缺漏、流程缺陷和数据隐私合规风险时触发此技能。 典型触发场景包括但不限于： - 企业委托律师或合规顾问进行内部合规风险评估 - 企业拟上市、并购或接受监管检查前进行合规自查 - 发生合规事件后，需要排查管理体系中的系统性缺陷 - 企业建立或更新合规管理体系时，需要评估现有制度与法律法规的匹配度 - 用户要求审查企业内部规章制度、核心业务流程或隐私政策协议的合规性 本技能覆盖三大审查维度：制度体系完整性审查、业务流程控制有效性审查、个人信息保护合规性审查。

Triggers when the enterprise's internal compliance management system needs a systematic review to identify policy gaps, process defects, and data-privacy compliance risks. Typical trigger scenarios include but are not limited to: the enterprise commissions lawyers or compliance consultants for an internal compliance risk assessment; compliance self-checks before a planned IPO, M&A deal, or regulatory inspection; investigating systemic defects in the management system after a compliance incident; assessing the fit between existing policies and laws and regulations when building or updating the compliance management system; the user asks to review the compliance of internal rules, core business processes, or privacy policy agreements. The skill covers three review dimensions: completeness review of the policy system, effectiveness review of business process controls, and compliance review of personal-information protection.
