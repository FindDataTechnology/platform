# criminal-trial-examination-outline

## What this is

庭审发问与举证质证提纲（识律刑事辩护套件原子技能）。一审阶段使用：当用户说"庭前会议""排非申请""非法证据排除""庭审发问""发问提纲""质证提纲""举证质证""交叉询问""逐份质证""辩方举证"或由 criminal-defense-workflow 调度时使用。输出庭前会议议程应对、非法证据排除申请、公诉人发问预判与应对、辩护人对被告人/证人/鉴定人的发问提纲、辩方举证提纲与逐份质证意见（书面版 + 庭上口述版）。

Court examination and evidence-challenge outlines (atomic skill of the 识律 criminal defense suite). For the first-instance stage: use when the user says 'pre-trial conference', 'suppression application', 'exclusion of illegal evidence', 'court examination', 'examination outline', 'evidence-challenge outline', 'evidence presentation and challenge', 'cross-examination', 'document-by-document challenge', 'defense presentation of evidence', or is dispatched by criminal-defense-workflow. Outputs pre-trial conference agenda responses, illegal-evidence exclusion applications, anticipation of and responses to prosecutor questions, defense examination outlines for the defendant/witnesses/expert witnesses, and the defense evidence plan with document-by-document challenge opinions (written version + courtroom spoken version).
