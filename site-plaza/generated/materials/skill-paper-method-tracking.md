# paper-method-tracking

## What this is

论文方法追踪（文本引擎·论文包）。当用户想查某个方法/技术（如 transformer、对比学习、GARCH）在学术论文里怎么用、有哪些变体、谁提出的，或要求"追一下 XX 方法的发展"时使用。按方法名驱动检索与脉络梳理，数据经 fd-text-core 引擎实时取。

Paper method tracking (text engine · papers pack). Use when the user wants to know how a method/technique (e.g. transformer, contrastive learning, GARCH) is used in academic papers, what variants exist, or who proposed it, or asks to 'trace the development of the XX method'. Drives retrieval and lineage mapping by method name; data fetched in real time via the fd-text-core engine.

- Species: skill (plaza category enum v1)
- Official: no
