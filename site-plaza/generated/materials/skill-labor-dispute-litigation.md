# labor-dispute-litigation

## What this is

劳动争议诉讼专业流程编排 Skill。整合17个子Skill：复用14个现有通用Skill + 3个劳动争议专属Skill（劳动争议仲裁程序管理、劳动关系认定与经济补偿计算、劳动争议证据体系）。用于判断劳动争议阶段（协商→调解→仲裁→一审→二审，L0-L15/LM系列）、识别W/E代理角色、区分劳动者代理、用人单位争议代理和用人单位日常劳动合规任务、推荐下一步子Skill、提示关键节点确认和期限；事项建档、路径、文件读取、来源披露、OCR 校正和缺口归档由「法律工作总控」统一处理。

Professional workflow-orchestration skill for labor dispute litigation. Integrates 17 sub-skills: reuses 14 existing general skills plus 3 labor-dispute-specific skills (labor dispute arbitration procedure management, labor relation determination and economic compensation calculation, and the labor dispute evidence system). Used to determine the labor dispute stage (negotiation → mediation → arbitration → first instance → second instance, L0-L15/LM series), identify W/E representation roles, distinguish worker representation, employer dispute representation, and employer routine labor compliance tasks, recommend the next sub-skill, and flag key-node confirmations and deadlines; matter filing, paths, file reading, source disclosure, OCR correction, and gap archiving are handled uniformly by the "legal work master controller".
