# mb-signal-retail

## What this is

零售行业信号包（谦面·生意体检姊妹件）：批发价参考（昆明花拍）、城市基本面（城市面板）、同业密度（工商登记，含个体工商户筛选）、快递大盘景气。工商数据批次 2023 滞后约 3 年，标「历史格局非实时」；无注销口径，关店量明示做不了。

Retail industry signal pack (Facet · business checkup companion): wholesale price reference (Kunming flower auction), city fundamentals (city panel), peer density (business registrations, with sole-proprietorship filtering), and overall express-delivery sentiment. The business-registration data is the 2023 batch, lagging about 3 years, marked 'historical pattern, not real-time'; with no deregistration basis, closure counts are explicitly stated as unavailable.

- Species: skill (plaza category enum v1)
- Official: no



## How to obtain

1. In 壹座 (Yizuo): Extensions → Skills — subscribe from the skills face.
2. Inside editors: skills reach your editor bundled with a pack that references them — install that pack with:

       npx @finddatatechnology/facet install <packId>

3. Deployed agent services receive registry skills through the registry supply channel.
