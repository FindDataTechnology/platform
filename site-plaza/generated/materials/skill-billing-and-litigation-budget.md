# billing-and-litigation-budget

## What this is

当用户需要统计、管理律师工时、专家费用、调查费用，控制诉讼成本、向客户出具工时单或费用单时使用此技能。 典型触发场景包括但不限于： - 提到"工作小时""专家费用""调查费用""办案成本""工时单""费用单""预算""成本控制"等关键词 - 询问某一律师的工作小时、某一案件的办案费用或诉讼成本时 - 用户要求交付律师工作时长总结或办案工时单、办案费用单，用于交付客户或申请报销时 - 需要对某一案件或常法服务进行预算编制、预算执行监控或预算偏差分析时 - 需要评估诉讼成本与预期收益，进行诉讼经济性分析时

Use this skill when the user needs to tally and manage attorney work hours, expert fees, and investigation costs; control litigation costs; or issue timesheets or fee statements to clients. Typical trigger scenarios include but are not limited to: mentioning keywords such as "work hours", "expert fees", "investigation costs", "case-handling costs", "timesheet", "fee statement", "budget", or "cost control"; asking about a given attorney's work hours or a given case's handling costs or litigation costs; requesting a summary of attorney time or case timesheets/fee statements for delivery to a client or for reimbursement claims; budgeting, budget-execution monitoring, or budget variance analysis for a case or for ongoing general legal services; and evaluating litigation costs against expected returns for a litigation economics analysis.
