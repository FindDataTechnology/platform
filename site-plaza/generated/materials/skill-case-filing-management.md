# case-filing-management

## What this is

系统化指引民事一审立案全流程，包括立案材料核查、管辖权预检、立案方式选择、诉讼费计算、举证期限管理等。当用户发送客户编号并提及"立案"、"去立案"、"立案准备"、"立案材料"、"网上立案"等指令时触发使用。

Systematically guides the full first-instance civil case-filing process, including filing material checks, jurisdiction pre-checks, filing method selection, court fee calculation, and evidence deadline management. Triggered when the user sends a client number and mentions commands such as 'file the case', 'go file', 'filing preparation', 'filing materials', or 'online filing'.

- Species: skill (plaza category enum v1)
- Official: no
