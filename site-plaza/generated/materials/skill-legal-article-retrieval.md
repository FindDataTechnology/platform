# legal-article-retrieval

## What this is

生成标准化法律检索报告，适用于案例检索、法条检索、类案分析等法律研究场景。当用户需要进行行业或专业分析、预测裁判结果、复核法律依据的有效性、确认请求、抗辩主张的法律依据、分析司法实践倾向等任务时使用。支持诉讼方案评估、庭审准备、合规审查、法律培训等各类法律实务场景。

Generates standardized legal research reports, suitable for case retrieval, statute retrieval, similar-case analysis, and other legal research scenarios. Use when the user needs to perform industry or professional analysis, predict adjudication outcomes, re-verify the validity of legal bases, confirm the legal grounds of claims or defenses, or analyze tendencies in judicial practice. Supports litigation plan evaluation, trial preparation, compliance review, legal training, and other legal practice scenarios.

- Species: skill (plaza category enum v1)
- Official: no

1. In 壹座 (Yizuo): Extensions → Skills — subscribe from the skills face.
2. Inside editors: skills reach your editor bundled with a pack that references them — install that pack with:

       npx @finddatatechnology/facet install <packId>

3. Deployed agent services receive registry skills through the registry supply channel.
