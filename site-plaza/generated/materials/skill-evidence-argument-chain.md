# evidence-argument-chain

## What this is

当需要将案件中的多项证据与法律论证主张进行系统性对应挂钩时触发此技能。 典型触发场景包括：准备诉讼文书（起诉状、答辩状、代理词）时需要构建完整的证据-主张对应关系； 庭审前整理证据目录并明确每项证据的证明目的；对方提出反驳后需要重新组织证据链以回应； 案件涉及多个争议焦点，需要将证据分组归类到不同论证主张下； 需要评估现有证据是否足以支撑全部主张，识别证据链中的薄弱环节和缺口。 本技能的核心是建立"主张→要件→证据→证明力评估"的完整映射关系，确保每个法律主张都有充分的证据支撑， 每项证据都有明确的证明目的，整体论证链条逻辑严密、层次清晰。

Triggers when multiple pieces of evidence in a case need to be systematically mapped to legal argument claims. Typical trigger scenarios include: building complete evidence-to-claim correspondences when preparing litigation documents (complaints, statements of defense, statements of representation); organizing the evidence list before trial and clarifying each item's probative purpose; reorganizing the evidence chain to respond after the opponent rebuts; grouping evidence under different argument claims when a case involves multiple disputed issues; assessing whether the existing evidence suffices to support all claims, and identifying weak links and gaps in the evidence chain. The core is establishing the complete mapping of "claim → element → evidence → probative-force assessment", ensuring every legal claim has sufficient evidentiary support and every item of evidence a clear probative purpose, with the overall argument chain logically tight and clearly layered.
