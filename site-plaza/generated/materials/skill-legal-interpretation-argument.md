# legal-interpretation-argument

## What this is

当法律推理过程中遇到以下情形时触发本技能： （1）法条文本含义模糊、存在多种理解可能； （2）法条适用于具体案件事实时产生争议； （3）需要论证某一法条应当/不应当适用于特定情形； （4）当事人双方对同一法条的理解存在分歧； （5）法条之间存在表面冲突，需要通过解释协调； （6）新型案件事实无法直接对应既有法条文本； （7）需要对裁判文书中的法律适用进行分析或质疑。 本技能的核心是运用文义解释、体系解释、目的解释等法律解释方法，对关键法条进行严谨的解释论证，得出合理、可辩护的法律含义，并以规范化的方式呈现解释过程与结论。

Triggers when the following situations arise during legal reasoning: (1) the meaning of a statutory text is ambiguous, with multiple possible readings; (2) applying a provision to concrete case facts is disputed; (3) it must be argued that a provision should/should not apply to a specific situation; (4) the two parties disagree on the understanding of the same provision; (5) provisions appear to conflict and need reconciliation through interpretation; (6) novel case facts cannot be directly mapped onto existing statutory texts; (7) the application of law in an adjudication document needs to be analyzed or challenged. The core of this skill is applying legal interpretation methods — literal, systemic, teleological, and others — to rigorously interpret and argue key provisions, reach reasonable and defensible legal meanings, and present the interpretive process and conclusions in a standardized way.
