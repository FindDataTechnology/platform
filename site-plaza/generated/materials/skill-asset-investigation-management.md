# asset-investigation-management

## What this is

R1（管理人）专属Skill，覆盖破产程序中的财产全面调查、可撤销/无效行为追回、财产管理方案编制。当用户发送客户编号并提及"财产调查"、"财产清查"、"可撤销行为"、"追回财产"、"财产管理方案"等指令时触发使用。

Skill exclusive to R1 (the administrator), covering comprehensive asset investigation in bankruptcy proceedings, recovery of voidable/invalid acts, and preparation of asset management plans. Triggered when the user sends a client number and mentions commands such as 'asset investigation', 'asset inventory', 'voidable acts', 'asset recovery', or 'asset management plan'.

- Species: skill (plaza category enum v1)
- Official: no
