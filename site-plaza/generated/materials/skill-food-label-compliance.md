# food-label-compliance

## What this is

预包装食品标签合规审核技能，用于审核食品标签是否符合 GB 7718（预包装食品标签通则）和 GB 28050（预包装食品营养标签通则）。适用场景：(1) 用户提交食品标签图片、文字或文档要求合规审核时；(2) 用户提到"食品标签审核""标签合规""营养标签审查""GB 7718""GB 28050"等关键词时；(3) 用户要求检查食品标签是否存在缺项、错误或违规风险时。支持 2011 版和 2025 版标准。

Prepackaged food label compliance review skill, used to review whether food labels conform to GB 7718 (General Standard for the Labeling of Prepackaged Foods) and GB 28050 (General Standard for Nutrition Labeling of Prepackaged Foods). Applicable when: (1) the user submits food label images, text, or documents for compliance review; (2) the user mentions keywords such as 'food label review', 'label compliance', 'nutrition label review', 'GB 7718', or 'GB 28050'; (3) the user asks to check food labels for missing items, errors, or violation risks. Supports both the 2011 and 2025 versions of the standards.
