# pretrial-preparation

## What this is

庭前准备。基于起诉状、法律分析、诉讼可视化、案例汇编、质证意见等前置文档，自动归纳争议焦点，生成争点导向型庭审提纲、质证攻防矩阵、举证策略规划表、开庭陈述提纲、法庭辩论提纲、代理词和诉讼法律服务方案。当用户发送客户编号并提及"庭前准备"、"庭审提纲"、"代理词"、"诉讼方案"等指令时触发使用。

Pretrial preparation. Based on preceding documents such as complaints, legal analyses, litigation visualizations, case compilations, and cross-examination opinions, it automatically summarizes disputed issues and generates issue-oriented trial outlines, evidence-challenge attack-and-defense matrices, evidence presentation strategy plans, opening statement outlines, courtroom debate outlines, representation statements, and litigation legal service plans. Triggers when the user sends a client number and mentions instructions such as "pretrial preparation", "trial outline", "representation statement", or "litigation plan".
