# legal-ocr

## What this is

本技能应在用户需要 OCR、扫描识别、图片文字识别、文档识别，或将 PDF、图片、Office 文档、URL 转换为 Markdown 时使用。检测到法律材料时可进行保守的法律术语与文书结构优化。不要用于法律事实判断、补写缺失内容、语义改写、印章深度识别或图表实体分析。

Use this skill when the user needs OCR, scan recognition, text recognition from images, document recognition, or conversion of PDFs, images, Office documents, or URLs into Markdown. When legal materials are detected, conservative optimization of legal terminology and document structure may be applied. Do not use for legal fact-finding, filling in missing content, semantic rewriting, deep seal recognition, or entity analysis of charts.

- Species: skill (plaza category enum v1)
- Official: no



## How to obtain

1. In 壹座 (Yizuo): Extensions → Skills — subscribe from the skills face.
2. Inside editors: skills reach your editor bundled with a pack that references them — install that pack with:

       npx @finddatatechnology/facet install <packId>

3. Deployed agent services receive registry skills through the registry supply channel.
