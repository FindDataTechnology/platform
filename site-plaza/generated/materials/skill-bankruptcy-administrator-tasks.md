# bankruptcy-administrator-tasks

## What this is

R1（管理人）专属Skill，覆盖管理人受理后的全部初期工作职责，包括印章与账户管理、财产接管、内部事务管理、合同处理、聘用工作人员、管理人团队管理及报酬方案制定。当用户发送客户编号并提及"管理人接管"、"开始接管"、"接管工作"等指令时触发使用。

Skill exclusive to R1 (the administrator), covering all initial post-acceptance duties of the administrator, including seal and account management, taking over assets, internal affairs management, contract handling, hiring personnel, administrator team management, and remuneration plan design. Triggered when the user sends a client number and mentions commands such as 'administrator takeover', 'start the takeover', or 'takeover work'.

- Official: no
