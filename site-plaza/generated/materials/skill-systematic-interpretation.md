# systematic-interpretation

## What this is

体系解释是指针对法律规范文义解释后所产生的含义或适用范围上的争议，根据法律规范在法律体系中的位置（如在哪一部法律、编、章或节），关联的法律规范的含义，或者法律作为一个体系的应由之意，做出最符合体系性的解释，从而明确体系视角下法律规范的含义与适用范围。 触发条件（满足任一即可调用）：

Systematic interpretation addresses disputes over meaning or scope of application that arise after the literal interpretation of a legal norm, producing the interpretation most consistent with the system — based on the norm's position within the legal system (which law, part, chapter, or section it belongs to), the meanings of the related legal norms, or the sense the law must bear as a system — thereby clarifying the meaning and scope of application of the norm from a systemic perspective. Trigger conditions (any one suffices to invoke):

- Species: skill (plaza category enum v1)
- Official: no
