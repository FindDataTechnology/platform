# statute-case-retrieval

## What this is

基于三层检索法进行系统性法律法规与案例检索，与前序"初步法律分析"请求权路径联动，输出法官版《法律法规及案例汇编》和律师版《法规案例检索报告》双文档，按总控当前事项记录和复盘台账衔接。当用户发送客户编号并提及"检索法规案例"、"法规汇编"、"案例检索"等指令时触发使用。

Performs systematic retrieval of statutes and case law based on a three-layer retrieval method, links with the claim-basis paths of the preceding preliminary legal analysis, and outputs two documents: the judge edition 'Statutes, Regulations and Case Compendium' and the lawyer edition 'Regulation and Case Retrieval Report', connected with the controller's current-matter record and review ledger. Triggered when the user sends a client number and mentions commands such as 'retrieve statutes and cases', 'statute compendium', or 'case retrieval'.
