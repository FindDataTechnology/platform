# strategic-risk-prioritization

## What this is

当法律分析中存在多个可能的结论、风险点或争议焦点时，需要对这些风险按照发生概率和影响程度进行系统化排序，以帮助决策者聚焦最关键的风险并合理分配资源。该技能在以下场景触发：(1)法律尽职调查中发现多项合规风险需要排序；(2)诉讼策略制定中需要评估多个不利结论的可能性与后果；(3)交易结构设计中需要识别并排列关键法律障碍；(4)合同审查中发现多个风险条款需要确定修改优先级；(5)监管合规评估中需要对多项违规风险进行分级管理；(6)任何需要在有限资源下对法律风险进行战略性取舍的场景。

When legal analysis contains multiple possible conclusions, risk points, or disputed issues, these risks need to be systematically ranked by probability of occurrence and degree of impact, helping decision-makers focus on the most critical risks and allocate resources sensibly. This skill triggers in the following scenarios: (1) multiple compliance risks found in legal due diligence that need ranking; (2) litigation strategy design requiring assessment of the likelihood and consequences of multiple adverse conclusions; (3) transaction structure design requiring identification and ordering of key legal obstacles; (4) multiple risky clauses found in contract review requiring determination of modification priorities; (5) regulatory compliance assessment requiring tiered management of multiple violation risks; (6) any scenario requiring strategic trade-offs among legal risks under limited resources.
