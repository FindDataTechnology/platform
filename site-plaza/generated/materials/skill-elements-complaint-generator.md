# elements-complaint-generator

## What this is

Use when converting 律师已写好的常规起诉状(md/docx)或对话描述为符合最高法 67 类官方要素式起诉状示范文本格式的 Word 文档(法〔2025〕82 号,2025-07-14 全国推广)。适用于民间借贷/离婚纠纷/机动车事故/劳动争议等所有 67 类民事/商事/行政/知产案由的要素式起诉状/答辩状生成。

Use when converting a lawyer's already-written ordinary complaint (md/docx) or a conversational description into a Word document conforming to the Supreme People's Court's 67 official element-based complaint model text formats (Fa [2025] No. 82, rolled out nationwide on 2025-07-14). Applies to generating element-based complaints/statements of defense for all 67 civil/commercial/administrative/IP causes of action such as private lending, divorce disputes, motor vehicle accidents, and labor disputes.

- Species: skill (plaza category enum v1)
- Official: no
