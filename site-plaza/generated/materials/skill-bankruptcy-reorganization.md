# bankruptcy-reorganization

## What this is

破产重整程序全流程管理。覆盖重整申请与受理、重整期间管理、重整计划草案编制、债权分组表决、法院强制批准、重整计划执行与监督等全部环节。适用于管理人（R1）、债权人代理（R2）、债务人代理（R3）三类角色。当用户发送客户编号并提及"重整"、"重整计划"、"重整程序"等指令时触发使用。

Full-process management of the bankruptcy reorganization procedure, covering all stages: reorganization application and acceptance, management during the reorganization period, drafting the reorganization plan, class voting by creditors, court compulsory approval, and execution and supervision of the reorganization plan. Applicable to the three roles of administrator (R1), creditor's counsel (R2), and debtor's counsel (R3). Triggered when the user sends a client number and mentions commands such as 'reorganization', 'reorganization plan', or 'reorganization procedure'.
