# evidence-card-organizer

## What this is

证据卡片提取与材料清单整理（立案材料整理技能）。当用户提交一批案件材料（合同、转账记录、聊天记录、票据、送达回证等），要求提取证据、编证据目录、整理材料清单或做证据关系与矛盾排查时使用。输出双视图证据卡片库与待补强清单，供起诉状/答辩状起草直接引用。

Evidence card extraction and material list organization (case-filing material organization skill). Use when the user submits a batch of case materials (contracts, transfer records, chat logs, receipts, proofs of service, etc.) and asks to extract evidence, compile an evidence list, organize material inventories, or screen evidence relationships and contradictions. Outputs a dual-view evidence card library and a to-strengthen list, ready for direct citation when drafting complaints/defense statements.

- Official: no
