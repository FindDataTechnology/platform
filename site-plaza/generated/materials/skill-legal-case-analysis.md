# legal-case-analysis

## What this is

本技能应在用户需要基于案件材料、咨询材料、合同资料、证据材料或检索结果进行法律分析、案件研判、风险评估、诉讼策略、刑事案件研判与审查起诉分析、非诉风险判断、客户说明或法律分析报告时使用。它是法律任务的前置分析引擎，不要求每次都生成正式报告。不要用于：单纯 OCR、单纯法条案例检索、单纯 Word 排版转换、仅需包装服务方案且无需新增法律分析的文档生成。

Use this skill when the user needs legal analysis, case assessment, risk evaluation, litigation strategy, criminal case assessment and review-for-prosecution analysis, non-litigation risk judgment, client explanations, or legal analysis reports based on case materials, consultation materials, contract documents, evidence, or retrieval results. It is the upstream analysis engine for legal tasks and does not require generating a formal report every time. Not for: OCR alone, statute/case retrieval alone, Word layout conversion alone, or document generation that only packages a service proposal without new legal analysis.
