# legal-qa-extractor

## What this is

从律师与客户沟通记录中提取有价值的法律问答对，生成结构化知识库内容。本技能应在用户需要整理客户咨询记录、从对话中提取可复用法律知识、创建问答知识库、或准备内容营销素材时使用。支持严格客户信息脱敏处理。

Extracts valuable legal Q&A pairs from lawyer-client communications to produce structured knowledge base content. Use this skill when the user needs to organize client consultation records, extract reusable legal knowledge from conversations, create Q&A knowledge bases, or prepare content marketing material. Supports strict client information de-identification.
