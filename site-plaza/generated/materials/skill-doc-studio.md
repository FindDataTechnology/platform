# doc-studio

## What this is

文档工坊——Word/Excel/PPT 商务文档生成与编辑。当用户要求生成报告、表格、演示文稿、文档交付物，或要求编辑/转换上传的 docx/xlsx/pptx 文件时使用。产出符合中式商务版式的成稿，生成后自动质检。

Document workshop — Word/Excel/PPT business document generation and editing. Use when the user asks to generate reports, spreadsheets, presentations, or document deliverables, or to edit/convert uploaded docx/xlsx/pptx files. Produces finished documents in Chinese-style business layout, with automatic quality checks after generation.
