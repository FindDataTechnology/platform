# judicial-value-judgment

## What this is

辅助法官在权利冲突、法律不确定、比例原则审查和裁量说理场景中，进行结构化、可审查、可论证的司法价值判断。 本 Skill 用于辅助法官在疑难案件中识别法律价值冲突、确定裁量边界、展开价值衡量，并形成可用于裁判说理的分析框架，不替代法官作出最终裁判，而是帮助法官将价值判断转化为有事实基础、规范基础和论证结构的司法推理过程。

Assists judges in making structured, reviewable, and arguable judicial value judgments in scenarios of rights conflicts, legal uncertainty, proportionality review, and discretionary reasoning. This Skill assists judges in difficult cases to identify legal value conflicts, determine the boundaries of discretion, conduct value weighing, and form an analytical framework usable for decision reasoning; it does not replace the judge in making the final ruling, but helps the judge convert value judgments into a judicial reasoning process with factual grounding, normative grounding, and argumentative structure.
