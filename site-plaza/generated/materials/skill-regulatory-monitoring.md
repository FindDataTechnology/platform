# regulatory-monitoring

## What this is

中国境内重点监管动态监测与客户合规影响评估 Skill。用于监管动态、新规更新、政策变化、行业监管、法规政策分级、客户合规缺口核查、政策制度修改建议、整改清单、监管合规简报等任务。适用于用户要求监测某行业、某客户、某产品或某主题的法律法规、监管政策、部门规章、规范性文件、征求意见稿、执法动态，并判断新规要求、客户现有合规文件缺口和整改方案。

Skill for monitoring key regulatory developments in mainland China and assessing their compliance impact on clients. Used for regulatory developments, new-rule updates, policy changes, industry regulation, tiering of statutes and policies, client compliance gap checks, policy/system revision recommendations, remediation checklists, and regulatory compliance briefings. Applicable when the user asks to monitor, for an industry, client, product, or topic, laws and regulations, regulatory policies, departmental rules, normative documents, draft rules for public comment, or enforcement developments, and to determine new-rule requirements, gaps in the client's existing compliance documents, and remediation plans.
