# legalskill-legal-consultation

## What this is

为律师、法务提供法条检索、法规查询、案例研究、判例检索、指导案例查找、司法解释解读、合同审查与专业研判，为普通公众提供法律咨询、案件分析和维权指导。适用于普通用户描述日常纠纷、追问法律后果或寻求行动策略，即使用户未使用法律术语，常见说法如欠钱不还、被起诉、打官司、合同违约、离婚、抚养权、抚养费、探视权、家暴、彩礼、遗产继承、劳动纠纷、工伤、加班费、年终奖、竞业限制、违法解除、违法辞退、裁员、被辞退、经济补偿、年休假、交通事故、车祸、租房押金不退、消费者投诉、公司股权争议、合伙纠纷、股权代持、股东知情权、拆迁补偿、行政处罚、行政复议、知识产权侵权、商标侵权、专利纠纷、著作权纠纷、商业秘密、不正当竞争、房屋买卖纠纷、物业纠纷、邻里纠纷、名誉权侵权、隐私泄露、网络暴力、医疗事故、医疗纠纷、保险合同纠纷、人身损害、被人打了、食品安全、产品责任等，或问"怎么办""能不能告""会不会坐牢""能赔多少""胜算大吗""有什么风险""要赔多少钱""会不会被拘留""会不会判刑""能不能缓刑""怎么应诉""如何反诉""如何上诉""打官司要多久""证据不足怎么办""对方不履行怎么办""律师费多少""仲裁和诉讼哪个好""要不要请律师""自己辩护行不行""调解书有法律效力吗""强制执行怎么申请""被列入失信名单怎么办""被当成老赖怎么办""对方转移财产怎么办""判决后不执行怎么办""取保候审要什么条件""能不能不起诉"等。也覆盖律师、法务的高频专业任务：按案号查案例、按裁判要旨找类案、法条沿革追溯、法条效力核实、合同条款分析与漏洞审查、违约赔偿计算、劳动关系认定、竞业限制审查、裁判口径对比与各地裁判标准差异分析、合规审查、诉讼风险评估、仲裁策略设计、调解方案拟订、强制执行与财产保全分析、刑事报案可行性初评、证据链梳理、庭审准备提纲、再审申请、执行异议、破产债权申报等。用户上传合同、判决书、起诉状、答辩状、仲裁申请书、执行申请书、律师函等法律文件时自动触发分析，支持多轮追问与策略细化。不适用于出具正式法律意见书、代写法律文书、代理出庭、学术写作或非中文法律检索。

Provides lawyers and in-house counsel with statute retrieval, regulation lookup, case study, precedent search, guiding-case lookup, judicial-interpretation analysis, contract review, and professional assessment; provides the general public with legal consultation, case analysis, and rights-protection guidance. Suits ordinary users describing everyday disputes, asking about legal consequences, or seeking action strategies, even when they use no legal terminology. Common phrasings include: owing money and not repaying, being sued, going to court, breach of contract, divorce, custody, child support, visitation rights, domestic violence, betrothal gifts (caili), inheritance, labor disputes, work injuries, overtime pay, year-end bonus, non-compete restrictions, unlawful termination, unlawful dismissal, layoffs, being dismissed, severance compensation, paid annual leave, traffic accidents, car crashes, unreturned rental deposits, consumer complaints, corporate equity disputes, partnership disputes, nominee shareholding, shareholders' right to information, demolition compensation, administrative penalties, administrative reconsideration, IP infringement, trademark infringement, patent disputes, copyright disputes, trade secrets, unfair competition, property sale disputes, property management disputes, neighborhood disputes, reputation infringement, privacy leaks, cyberbullying, medical malpractice, medical disputes, insurance contract disputes, personal injury, being assaulted, food safety, product liability, and the like; or questions such as "What should I do?", "Can I sue?", "Will I go to jail?", "How much compensation can I get?", "Are my chances good?", "What are the risks?", "How much do I have to pay?", "Will I be detained?", "Will I be sentenced?", "Can I get probation?", "How do I respond to the suit?", "How do I counterclaim?", "How do I appeal?", "How long does a lawsuit take?", "What if the evidence is insufficient?", "What if the other party does not perform?", "How much are attorney fees?", "Arbitration or litigation — which is better?", "Do I need a lawyer?", "Can I defend myself?", "Is a mediation agreement legally binding?", "How do I apply for compulsory enforcement?", "What if I am placed on the dishonesty list?", "What if I am treated as a deadbeat debtor?", "What if the other party transfers assets away?", "What if the judgment is not enforced?", "What are the conditions for release on bail pending trial?", "Can there be no prosecution?", and the like. It also covers the high-frequency professional tasks of lawyers and in-house counsel: finding cases by case number, finding similar cases by holding, tracing the revision history of provisions, verifying provision validity, contract clause analysis and loophole review, breach-damages calculation, labor-relationship determination, non-compete review, comparison of adjudication approaches and analysis of regional differences in adjudication standards, compliance review, litigation risk assessment, arbitration strategy design, drafting mediation plans, enforcement and asset-preservation analysis, preliminary feasibility assessment of criminal complaints, evidence-chain mapping, trial-preparation outlines, retrial applications, enforcement objections, bankruptcy claim filing, and more. Analysis is triggered automatically when the user uploads legal documents such as contracts, judgments, complaints, statements of defense, arbitration applications, enforcement applications, or attorney letters; multi-turn follow-up questions and strategy refinement are supported. Not suitable for issuing formal legal opinions, ghost-writing legal documents, court representation, academic writing, or non-Chinese legal research.
