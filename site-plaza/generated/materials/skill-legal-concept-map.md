# legal-concept-map

## What this is

法律概念结构化图解。当用户要解释某个法律概念/制度（"什么是善意取得""表见代理怎么认定""情势变更的适用条件"）、要拆构成要件与例外、要找关联条文与权威解读时使用。以条级引用解析锚定条文，以五源语料补权威解读，输出要件—例外—关联条文的结构化图解。

Structured diagramming of legal concepts. Use when the user wants an explanation of a legal concept or institution ('what is good-faith acquisition', 'how is apparent agency determined', 'conditions for applying change of circumstances'), wants constituent elements and exceptions broken down, or wants related provisions and authoritative interpretations. Anchors provisions via article-level citation parsing, supplements authoritative interpretations from the five-source corpus, and outputs a structured map of elements—exceptions—related provisions.
