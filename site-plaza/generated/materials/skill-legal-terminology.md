# legal-terminology

## What this is

当AI智能体在生成、审查或修改任何法律文本（包括但不限于合同、诉状、法律意见书、裁判文书、法规释义、法律备忘录等）时，需要确保其中使用的法律术语措辞准确、无歧义、符合法律文体要求。该技能在以下场景触发： 1. 起草或修改法律文书时，需要选择正确的法律术语； 2. 审查法律文本时，发现术语使用不规范、混淆或存在歧义； 3. 将日常用语转化为法律规范表达时； 4. 翻译或对照中外法律术语时； 5. 用户明确要求对法律文本进行术语规范化处理时； 6. 对法律概念进行精确定义或解释时。 本技能是法律写作的基础原子能力，贯穿所有法律文本生产环节。

When the AI agent generates, reviews, or modifies any legal text (including but not limited to contracts, pleadings, legal opinions, adjudication documents, statute annotations, and legal memoranda), it must ensure that the legal terminology used is worded accurately, without ambiguity, and in line with legal style requirements. This skill triggers in the following scenarios: 1. selecting the correct legal terms when drafting or revising legal documents; 2. detecting non-standard, confused, or ambiguous terminology when reviewing legal texts; 3. converting everyday language into normative legal expression; 4. translating or aligning Chinese and foreign legal terms; 5. the user explicitly requests terminology normalization of a legal text; 6. precisely defining or explaining a legal concept. This skill is the foundational atomic capability of legal writing and runs through all stages of legal text production.
