# legal-workflow-controller

## What this is

legal 文件夹通用入口 Skill。用于法律咨询、案件办理、合同、产品法务、监管合规、诉讼、刑辩、劳动争议、破产、合规、文书、检索等任务的语义路由、案件隔离、来源披露、文件读取复查、法规/Wiki 校验、OCR 校正、缺口提示和合同偏好学习。用户提出任何法律工作请求、客户编号、案件材料处理、法律文书生成或需要自动匹配 legal 子 Skill 时触发。

General entry-point skill for the legal folder. Provides semantic routing, case isolation, source disclosure, file-read re-checking, statute/Wiki verification, OCR correction, gap flagging, and contract preference learning for tasks such as legal consultation, case handling, contracts, product legal affairs, regulatory compliance, litigation, criminal defense, labor disputes, bankruptcy, compliance, document drafting, and retrieval. Triggered when the user makes any legal work request, provides a client number, requests case material processing or legal document generation, or needs automatic matching to a legal sub-skill.

- Species: skill (plaza category enum v1)
- Official: no



## How to obtain

1. In 壹座 (Yizuo): Extensions → Skills — subscribe from the skills face.
2. Inside editors: skills reach your editor bundled with a pack that references them — install that pack with:

       npx @finddatatechnology/facet install <packId>

3. Deployed agent services receive registry skills through the registry supply channel.
