# patent-analysis

## What this is

面向中国发明和实用新型专利的结构化初步分析工具。本技能应在用户需要拆解或解释权利要求、比较专利保护范围、进行产品侵权比对、等同分析、稳定性与无效风险分析、FTO、规避设计、专利价值评估或制作专利分析图表时使用。不要用于：代替专利律师或专利代理师出具正式意见、仅下载/OCR专利、撰写专利申请文件，或在未确认法域与有效权利要求时给出确定性结论；外观设计近似判断需另行采用专门方法并人工复核。

A structured preliminary analysis tool for Chinese invention and utility model patents. Use this skill when the user needs to break down or interpret claims, compare patent protection scopes, perform product infringement comparisons, equivalence analysis, stability and invalidation risk analysis, FTO, design-arounds, patent value assessment, or produce patent analysis charts. Not for: replacing formal opinions of patent lawyers or patent agents, merely downloading/OCR-ing patents, drafting patent application documents, or giving definitive conclusions without confirming the jurisdiction and valid claims; design-similarity judgment for design patents requires a dedicated method and manual re-check.
