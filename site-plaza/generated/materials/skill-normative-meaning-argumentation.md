# normative-meaning-argumentation

## What this is

分析法律规范的目的、价值导向，以及事实应如何进入规范评价，判断涵摄的限度，需要适当区别于法律解释中的规范目的解释。

Analyzes the purpose and value orientation of legal norms, and how facts should enter normative evaluation, determining the limits of subsumption; must be appropriately distinguished from teleological (normative-purpose) interpretation within legal interpretation.
