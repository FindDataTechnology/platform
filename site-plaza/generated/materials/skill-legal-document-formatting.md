# legal-document-formatting

## What this is

基于中国人民法院裁判文书制作规范，起草完整的民事判决书或刑事判决书。当用户要求"起草判决书"、"生成裁判文书"、"写民事判决书"、"写刑事判决书"、"根据案卷写文书"或提供案卷材料要求输出裁判文书时使用本技能，在起草各段落时自主调用原子技能（如争议焦点识别、法条检索、演绎推理、证据效力评估等）。

Drafts complete civil or criminal judgments based on the adjudication-document standards of the Chinese People's Courts. Use when the user asks to "draft a judgment", "generate an adjudication document", "write a civil judgment", "write a criminal judgment", "write the document from the case file", or provides case-file material requesting an adjudication document; while drafting each section, it autonomously invokes atomic skills (such as disputed-issue identification, statute retrieval, deductive reasoning, and evidence-validity assessment).
