# special-criminal-procedures

## What this is

刑事特殊程序辩护与代理Skill，覆盖违法所得没收程序、精神病人强制医疗程序、再审申诉程序的启动条件、材料审查、听证或开庭准备、文书生成和与各刑事阶段Skill衔接。当用户提及"违法所得没收"、"强制医疗"、"精神病鉴定"、"再审"、"申诉"、"生效判决错误"等关键词时触发使用。

Skill for defense and representation in special criminal procedures, covering the initiation conditions, material review, hearing or trial preparation, document generation, and hand-off to the stage-specific criminal skills for the confiscation-of-illegal-gains procedure, the compulsory medical treatment procedure for the mentally ill, and the retrial/petition procedure. Triggered when the user mentions keywords such as 'confiscation of illegal gains', 'compulsory medical treatment', 'psychiatric assessment', 'retrial', 'petition', or 'effective judgment errors'.

- Species: skill (plaza category enum v1)
- Official: no
