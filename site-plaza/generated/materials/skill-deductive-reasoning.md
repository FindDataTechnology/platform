# deductive-reasoning

## What this is

基于形式逻辑执行严密的法律演绎推理。当用户要求"分析案件逻辑"、"验证法律推理"、"提取三段论"、"梳理案件事实与法条的关系"或"判断法律论证的有效性"时使用本技能。 为用户提供基于形式逻辑的法律推理框架，将非结构化的法律规范与案件事实转化为有效、可检验、可复现的演绎论证链条（P-F-C）。

Performs rigorous legal deductive reasoning based on formal logic. Use this skill when the user asks to "analyze case logic", "verify legal reasoning", "extract syllogisms", "map the relationship between case facts and legal provisions", or "judge the validity of a legal argument". Provides the user with a formal-logic-based legal reasoning framework, converting unstructured legal norms and case facts into valid, testable, reproducible deductive argument chains (P-F-C).
