# bankruptcy-reconciliation

## What this is

提供破产和解程序全流程支持，覆盖从和解申请、协议草案编制、债权人会议表决、协议效力确认到执行监督及转清算的完整流程。适用《企业破产法》第95-99条。当用户发送客户编号并提及"破产和解"、"和解协议"、"和解程序"等指令时触发使用。

Provides full-process support for the bankruptcy composition procedure, covering the complete flow from the composition application, drafting of the agreement, creditor meeting voting, and confirmation of the agreement's validity, through execution supervision and conversion to liquidation. Applies Articles 95-99 of the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law. Triggered when the user sends a client number and mentions commands such as 'bankruptcy composition', 'composition agreement', or 'composition procedure'.
