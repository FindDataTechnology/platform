# legal-harness-init

## What this is

面向法律工作者初始化或增量治理 AGENTS.md/CLAUDE.md：识别当前 AI harness，区分用户级、项目级和团队级指令，生成最小法律安全基线，安全合并受管区块，并在新会话中验证权限、保密、信息缺口和回溯行为。用户明确提到法律工作且要配置 harness、AGENTS.md、CLAUDE.md、agent 协作规则或 AI 使用基线时使用。不要用于合同审查、案件分析、文书起草、项目脚手架或 Skill 开发。

Initialize or incrementally govern AGENTS.md/CLAUDE.md for legal practitioners: identify the current AI harness, distinguish user-level, project-level, and team-level instructions, generate a minimal legal safety baseline, safely merge managed blocks, and verify permissions, confidentiality, information gaps, and trace-back behavior in a new session. Use when the user explicitly mentions legal work and wants to configure a harness, AGENTS.md, CLAUDE.md, agent collaboration rules, or an AI usage baseline. Do not use for contract review, case analysis, document drafting, project scaffolding, or skill development.
