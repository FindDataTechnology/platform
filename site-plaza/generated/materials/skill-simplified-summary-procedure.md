# simplified-summary-procedure

## What this is

刑事辩护简易程序、速裁程序及普通程序简化审理分支Skill，重点处理认罪认罚案件中的程序适用审查、量刑协商、庭审简化、权利保障和时间节点管理。作为一审阶段辩护的分支，当用户提及"简易程序"、"速裁程序"、"认罪认罚"、"快速审理"、"当庭宣判"等关键词时触发使用。

Branch skill for criminal defense in the simplified procedure, the expedited procedure, and streamlined trial under the ordinary procedure, focusing on procedural-applicability review, sentencing negotiation, trial simplification, rights protection, and time-node management in guilty-plea cases. As a branch of first-instance defense work, it is triggered when the user mentions keywords such as 'simplified procedure', 'expedited procedure', 'guilty plea and acceptance of punishment', 'expedited trial', or 'same-day judgment'.
