# other-legal-retrieval

## What this is

当智能体需要检索法律条文、司法解释、典型案例之外的辅助性法律信息时触发此技能。具体包括但不限于：立法背景资料（草案说明、审议报告、立法机关答记者问）、监管案例与行政处罚决定、地方指导意见与会议纪要、部门规章的起草说明与政策解读、行业标准与技术规范、法律实施情况报告、人大常委会执法检查报告、白皮书与政策文件、学术通说与权威教材观点、域外法律比较资料等。当用户问题涉及'为什么这样规定''立法目的是什么''实践中监管部门怎么处理''地方上有什么特殊规定''行业惯例是什么'等语境时，应当触发本技能。

Triggers when the agent needs to retrieve auxiliary legal information beyond statutes, judicial interpretations, and typical cases. Specifically includes but is not limited to: legislative background materials (draft explanations, deliberation reports, legislators' press Q&A), regulatory enforcement cases and administrative penalty decisions, local guidance documents and meeting minutes, drafting explanations and policy readings of departmental rules, industry standards and technical specifications, law-implementation status reports, NPC standing-committee law-enforcement inspection reports, white papers and policy documents, academic prevailing views and authoritative textbook positions, comparative foreign-law materials, and more. This skill should be triggered when the user's question involves contexts such as "why is it regulated this way", "what is the legislative purpose", "how do regulators handle it in practice", "what special local provisions exist", or "what are the industry conventions".

- Species: skill (plaza category enum v1)
- Official: no



## How to obtain

1. In 壹座 (Yizuo): Extensions → Skills — subscribe from the skills face.
2. Inside editors: skills reach your editor bundled with a pack that references them — install that pack with:

       npx @finddatatechnology/facet install <packId>

3. Deployed agent services receive registry skills through the registry supply channel.
