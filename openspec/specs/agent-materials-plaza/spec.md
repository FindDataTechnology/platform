# Agent Materials Plaza Specification

## Purpose

The Materials Plaza is the outward understanding surface of 壹座·智能体素材广场: a static five-locale site plus machine-readable surfaces that let humans of any language background and AI agents alike understand what infrastructure the platform provides, how it is provided, and which materials exist today — rendered from the registries' own public faces, with the material inventory itself as the site's core.

## Requirements

### Requirement: The human face is a five-locale static site with a fixed narrative structure

The plaza's human face SHALL be an entry view rendered by the facet web shell at its hash root `#/`,
serving five locales (zh-CN, en, es, fr, ja) with locale parity enforced by the shell's locale gate.
It SHALL NO LONGER be a standalone static site with its own root path: the supply domain has one
product address, and the entry view is part of that shell, not a second page competing for `/`.

The entry view SHALL present, in order: the material narrative (what a material is, the five
species as one card each with a concrete example); the supply mechanism chain in four segments
(publish → obtain → run → serve), each explained from the material's perspective; the product's
team-template and creation-path narrative; and the dual audience section (human paths and AI paths).
No locale SHALL fall back to another locale's text.

The entry view SHALL NOT embed the full inventory. It MAY carry a **bounded preview** that counts the
species and routes into the catalog; it MUST NOT render a species' full list. The inventory itself
lives in the shell's single catalog view (`#/catalog`), which carries the per-species tabs, the
official/facade badges, the category filtering, the search, and the per-species counts.

A preview entry's display name and its one-sentence description SHALL be carried in every locale the
export serves, and the shell SHALL render the entry in the reader's current locale. One preview block
SHALL NOT mix languages: a species whose copy exists in the requested locale SHALL NOT fall back to
another locale's sentence while a sibling entry on the same card renders in the requested locale.

The narrative sections SHALL be static. A viewer MUST be able to read the whole narrative and reach
the rest of the shell with every catalog source unreachable — the entry view SHALL NOT depend on any
material fetch to render.

#### Scenario: Five locales render complete pages

- **WHEN** a visitor opens the entry view in each of the five locales
- **THEN** every section renders fully in the requested locale, and the locale gate reports key parity across all five

#### Scenario: The narrative reads without a catalog

- **WHEN** every catalog source is unreachable or slow
- **THEN** the narrative sections and the shell navigation render identically, and no section of the entry view is missing or empty

#### Scenario: The mechanism chain explains infrastructure through materials

- **WHEN** a reader follows the supply mechanism section
- **THEN** each of the four segments states what the mechanism does for the materials (e.g. publish freezes an immutable version snapshot) rather than presenting an architecture lecture

#### Scenario: Hero carries the species inventory

- **WHEN** the entry view's preview loads with a valid source
- **THEN** it shows the per-species counts (agents/packs, skills, MCP services, facades, official data) and the tool count, not only a single pack count

#### Scenario: Species tab filters the gallery

- **WHEN** a visitor selects the skills or MCP species tab in the catalog
- **THEN** the catalog narrows to that species and the other species' cards are hidden, with counts per tab visible

#### Scenario: Hostname preselects the species

- **WHEN** a visitor arrives through `skills.finddatatech.cloud`
- **THEN** the redirect carries the skills species preselection and the catalog opens on it, while the bare canonical address opens the entry view unfiltered

#### Scenario: Preview entries speak the reader's language

- **WHEN** a visitor opens the catalog's 全部 tab in a non-Chinese locale
- **THEN** every preview entry's name and sentence render in that locale, and no entry on the page renders in a different language from its siblings

#### Scenario: A material link still resolves

- **WHEN** a reader follows any material's self-contained page link from the catalog
- **THEN** that page is served at its unchanged address, independent of which view renders the link

### Requirement: Machine surfaces are generated from the registries' own public faces

The plaza's machine surfaces — `llms.txt`, `llms-full.txt`, `catalog.json`, per-material Markdown pages, and `infrastructure.md` — SHALL be generated by the plaza export from the registries' own public faces (the pack listing, the skills catalog, the MCP catalog and its per-server depth). No hand-maintained inventory copy SHALL exist: if a material appears in the machine surfaces, it comes from its registry; if it was unpublished or group-gated away, the next export drops it. `catalog.json` entries SHALL reuse the production annotation shape (`canonical.brief`, `human` in five locales, `whenToUse`, `provenance.source` + `provenance.updatedAt`) with no new vocabulary; MCP entries carry their annotation-layer projection natively, and skill entries not yet covered by an annotation pass SHALL carry their zh source text in all five locale slots with provenance unchanged rather than being silently translated or omitted. Every human page SHALL embed JSON-LD structured data and a link to its machine twin.

The supply-domain front door host SHALL serve the machine surfaces at their canonical root paths — `/llms.txt`, `/llms-full.txt`, `/catalog.json`, `/robots.txt` — from the same process that serves the supply catalog API, and the deployment SHALL verify those four paths return their own file content after every deploy. A machine path SHALL NOT be answered with the human shell or any other HTML document. A host that owns a machine surface SHALL answer it with that surface's own bytes or, when the surface is unavailable, with an explicit error status; it MUST NOT answer with a soft success carrying HTML, because a soft 200 is indistinguishable from a real machine face to a crawler. A host that owns no machine surface SHALL answer 404 for it.

Every material count printed on a machine surface SHALL be derived from the supply species enumeration and SHALL name its species explicitly. A composite label that folds several species into one number (for example an "agents" total spanning packs, facades and data anchors) SHALL NOT appear on a machine surface.

#### Scenario: Catalog entries carry the annotation shape

- **WHEN** an AI agent reads `catalog.json`
- **THEN** every entry has a canonical brief, human summaries in five locale slots, a whenToUse hint, and provenance (source and as-of date), using the same field names as the production annotation layer

#### Scenario: MCP entries speak the annotation layer natively

- **WHEN** an annotated MCP server is exported with a requested locale
- **THEN** its catalog entry carries the projected localized name/summary/whenToUse from the supply annotations, falling back to zh exactly as the platform's own surfaces do

#### Scenario: Un-annotated skills are honest about their text

- **WHEN** a skill has no annotation coverage
- **THEN** its entry carries the zh source text (not a machine translation, not an omission) and its provenance still names the skills catalog and as-of date

#### Scenario: Unpublish removes a material from the next export

- **WHEN** a listed pack is unpublished on the market and the export runs again
- **THEN** the material disappears from `catalog.json`, `llms.txt`, and the materials pages, while previously deployed static files keep serving until the next deploy (stale copy visibly older in its provenance date)

#### Scenario: Human pages carry machine twins

- **WHEN** a crawler or AI agent parses any plaza human page
- **THEN** it finds JSON-LD describing the page and a link to the corresponding machine-readable surface

#### Scenario: Root machine paths serve their own file

- **WHEN** an agent requests `/llms.txt`, `/llms-full.txt`, `/catalog.json` or `/robots.txt` on the supply-domain front door host
- **THEN** the response body is that surface's own content with a matching content type, served by the same process that answers the supply catalog API, never an HTML document

#### Scenario: An unavailable machine surface is an error, not the shell

- **WHEN** the front door host is asked for a machine path but the generated surface is missing or unreadable
- **THEN** the response is an explicit error status with no HTML body, and it is never a 200 carrying the SPA shell

#### Scenario: A host without machine faces answers 404

- **WHEN** a machine path is requested on a host that owns no machine surface
- **THEN** the response is 404, not a soft success carrying a human shell

#### Scenario: Counts name their species

- **WHEN** any machine surface prints inventory counts
- **THEN** each count is labeled with a species name from the supply species enumeration, and no merged multi-species total appears

### Requirement: Each species admits through its own registry's public face

The plaza export SHALL admit each species through that species' own source-of-truth public face — packs through the market listing rule (public, non-unlisted, curated — or the operator whitelist replacing curation outright), skills and MCP servers through the registry visibility rule (no required groups = public; group-gated entries never appear anonymously), facades through the deployments public face (only publicly shared facades admit; instance status reported by the serving plane is included when present and rendered as an explicit unknown when absent), and data anchors through the DAAS catalog face (v1 scope: catalog classes; provenance names the DAAS catalog). The export SHALL NOT restate or reinterpret any admission predicate, and SHALL NOT derive a species from another species' badges, names, or category strings. A reconciliation gate SHALL compare the exported catalog's per-species material count with each source's count at export time and fail the build on any mismatch.

#### Scenario: Private, unlisted, and group-gated materials never appear

- **WHEN** the export runs against sources containing private packs, unlisted packs, group-gated MCP servers, and non-public facades
- **THEN** none of them appear in any machine surface or human gallery — byte-identical to what the anonymous browse faces show

#### Scenario: Per-source count reconciliation gate

- **WHEN** any species' exported count differs from its source's count at export time
- **THEN** the export fails naming the species and both counts, and no artifacts are written

#### Scenario: Badge-derived species entries are gone

- **WHEN** a pack carries a facade/assistant badge or a data-ish name but its species' own source has no corresponding record
- **THEN** no facade or data-anchor entry is derived from that pack, and the reconciliation gate counts only source-admitted entries

### Requirement: The inventory spans the three material species

The export SHALL ingest all five material species: packs from the market listing, skills from the skills catalog, MCP servers from the MCP catalog, facades from the deployments public face, and data anchors from the DAAS catalog face. Each MCP server's material page SHALL embed its full public tool surface (tool names and descriptions, tool count, license and provider where exposed) rather than splitting tools into separate pages. Each pack's material page SHALL enumerate the skills and MCP server references declared in its own manifest, so the contents inside 壹座 packs are themselves visible. Each facade's material page SHALL state its deployment provenance and its live status as reported (or an explicit unknown mark). Each data anchor's material page SHALL state its DAAS catalog provenance and as-of date.

#### Scenario: Skills and MCP species are populated

- **WHEN** the export completes with all five sources reachable
- **THEN** `catalog.json` carries nonzero entries for all five species — packs, skills, MCP servers, facades, data anchors — each with a stable species-appropriate address, and the hero counts agree with the catalog

#### Scenario: Facade and data-anchor species are populated by their sources

- **WHEN** the export completes with all five sources reachable
- **THEN** `catalog.json` carries facade and data-anchor entries admitted by their own source faces alongside packs, skills, and MCP servers, each with a stable species-appropriate address

#### Scenario: MCP material page embeds the tool surface

- **WHEN** a reader opens an MCP server's material page
- **THEN** the page lists every public tool with its description and the total tool count, self-contained with provenance

#### Scenario: Pack page enumerates its own contents

- **WHEN** a reader opens a pack's material page
- **THEN** the page names the skills and MCP server references the pack's manifest declares, in addition to the obtain paths

#### Scenario: Facade page is honest about status

- **WHEN** a reader opens a facade's material page whose live status has not been reported
- **THEN** the page renders an explicit unknown status mark rather than a defaulted value

### Requirement: Freshness is always visible and degrade is graceful

The inventory gallery SHALL attempt a live refresh from the market listing API on page load and SHALL silently fall back to the build-time snapshot when the API is unreachable, always labeling which it is showing ("as of <date>" for the snapshot). A page with a valid snapshot SHALL never render an empty inventory because the live fetch failed. The snapshot file SHALL be written at export time and carry its generation date for both the fallback face and audit.

The export SHALL run inside the supply-domain service on a bounded cadence independent of human deploys, and SHALL additionally run on a publish event, so the machine surfaces cannot drift indefinitely behind the live market. Each machine surface SHALL carry its generation timestamp, and the export SHALL fail rather than publish a surface whose per-species counts disagree with the source registries at export time. A failed export SHALL leave the previously generated surfaces serving unchanged and SHALL record the failure where an operator can see it; it MUST NOT leave a partially written surface set in place.

#### Scenario: Live refresh succeeds

- **WHEN** the gallery loads and the market API responds
- **THEN** the inventory reflects the live listing and is not labeled as snapshot

#### Scenario: Live refresh fails to snapshot

- **WHEN** the market API is unreachable from a visitor's browser
- **THEN** the gallery renders the build-time snapshot labeled with its as-of date, and no error blocks the page

#### Scenario: Machine surfaces refresh without a deploy

- **WHEN** the market gains a pack and no human deploys the plaza
- **THEN** the scheduled in-service export regenerates the machine surfaces and their generation timestamps advance

#### Scenario: Stale export cannot publish

- **WHEN** the export's per-species counts disagree with a source registry at export time
- **THEN** the export fails, the previously generated surfaces keep serving with their older timestamp, and the failure is visible to an operator

#### Scenario: A failed export leaves no half-written surface set

- **WHEN** an export run aborts partway through writing the surface set
- **THEN** the previously generated surfaces remain intact and complete, and no visitor or agent sees a mixed-generation surface set

### Requirement: AI-readable discipline is gated, not aspirational

The export SHALL enforce six lint rules on every generated machine surface: each section self-contained (no cross-page or cross-section references like "see above"); full entity naming on first mention (壹座·智能体素材广场 / Yizuo Agent Materials Plaza, not "our platform"); one term per concept (素材 never mixed with 资源/内容/资产 in the same meaning); mechanisms expressed as input–action–output tables; every material page independently answerable by a context-free reader to three questions (what is this, how do I obtain it, as of when); and provenance present on every material entry. A lint failure SHALL fail the export build.

#### Scenario: Cold-read three-question assertion

- **WHEN** a material page alone is given to a reader with no other context
- **THEN** the page answers what the material is, how to obtain it (with the copy-ready command or path), and the corpus-as-of date

#### Scenario: Lint failure blocks the build

- **WHEN** a generated page contains a cross-section reference or a missing provenance field
- **THEN** the export exits non-zero naming the offending page and rule, and no artifacts are written

### Requirement: Addresses are stable across content and locale changes

Material page slugs SHALL be stable identifiers that do not change when content updates or locales are added; locale switching SHALL NOT alter material addresses. Slugs SHALL be namespaced per species (pack-, skill-, mcp-) so the three registries never collide. The `catalog.json` category field SHALL be a versioned closed enumeration; adding a new material species SHALL extend the enumeration with a version bump rather than overloading an existing category.

#### Scenario: Content update keeps the address

- **WHEN** a material's corpus is updated and re-exported
- **THEN** its page URL is unchanged, and prior inbound references (from AI agents or other sites) still resolve

#### Scenario: New species extends the enumeration

- **WHEN** a new material species (e.g. fleet templates) joins the plaza
- **THEN** the category enumeration gains a new value with a version bump, and no existing category absorbs it

### Requirement: Three domains open one inventory

The supply domain SHALL present one inventory through one canonical front door — `facet.finddatatech.cloud` —
and the remaining historical hostnames SHALL be compatibility doors over that same inventory, never a second
product face:

- `agent.finddatatech.cloud` — a compatibility door for everything already pointing at it. An HTML request
  SHALL redirect to the canonical front door. The machine surfaces (`/llms.txt`, `/llms-full.txt`,
  `/catalog.json`, `/robots.txt`) and per-material pages (`/materials/*`) SHALL be served with their own bytes
  and HTTP 200 from this host, without a redirect: those addresses are already published to AI crawlers and
  embedded material links, and a redirect would both add a hop to every crawl and turn a stable machine
  address into an unstable one.
- `skills.finddatatech.cloud` — redirect to the canonical front door with the skills species preselected, for a
  bounded compatibility window.
- `mcp.finddatatech.cloud` — the execution plane's host. Its protocol endpoints (`/<server>/mcp`), API, and
  health paths SHALL be served unchanged; its anonymous root SHALL redirect to the canonical front door. The
  registry console on this host is an operator surface, not a product entrance.

The canonical front door SHALL serve the entry view at its root. It SHALL NOT serve a second, standalone
human page at `/`: the shell owns the root, the entry view is one of its hash-addressed views, and every
other shell view SHALL remain reachable at its own hash address.

All hostnames SHALL be covered by the edge certificate (single SAN set; a re-sign that drops any prior domain
is a failure).

#### Scenario: The canonical front door serves the whole supply domain

- **WHEN** a visitor opens the canonical front door without a hash
- **THEN** the entry view renders inside the shell, and the shell's other views are reachable from it

#### Scenario: Every shell view stays reachable

- **WHEN** a signed-in user opens any of the shell's own views — the storefront, the creator console, the curation board, the studio, the docs, the fleet view — by address or by navigation
- **THEN** each renders; a root-path static page SHALL NOT shadow them

#### Scenario: An HTML request to a compatibility door redirects

- **WHEN** an anonymous client requests an HTML page on `agent.finddatatech.cloud`
- **THEN** it is redirected to the canonical front door

#### Scenario: A machine surface on a compatibility door does not redirect

- **WHEN** a client requests `/llms.txt`, `/llms-full.txt`, `/catalog.json` or `/robots.txt` on
  `agent.finddatatech.cloud`
- **THEN** the response is HTTP 200 carrying that surface's own bytes with a matching content type, with no
  redirect

#### Scenario: A material deep link survives on the compatibility door

- **WHEN** an already-published `/materials/<slug>` URL is requested on `agent.finddatatech.cloud`
- **THEN** the page is served with HTTP 200 and its content, with no redirect

#### Scenario: Skills door preselects the species

- **WHEN** a visitor opens `skills.finddatatech.cloud`
- **THEN** they are redirected to the canonical front door with the skills species preselected, and can clear
  the filter to see everything

#### Scenario: MCP anonymous root redirects, protocol endpoints never

- **WHEN** an anonymous client requests the root of `mcp.finddatatech.cloud`
- **THEN** it is redirected to the canonical front door, while a request to `/<server>/mcp` or `/api/*` on the
  same host is served by the registry unchanged

#### Scenario: Registry session keeps its console

- **WHEN** a client with a valid registry session requests the root of `mcp.finddatatech.cloud`
- **THEN** the registry console answers as its own spec requires — the redirect only governs anonymous traffic

#### Scenario: Certificate covers every door

- **WHEN** the edge certificate is re-signed for the supply domain's hostnames
- **THEN** its SAN list includes every prior domain plus the new ones, and a TLS handshake succeeds for all of
  them

#### Scenario: One brand across every door

- **WHEN** a client reads the brand string served from any of the four hostnames
- **THEN** it names the same product, and no host serves a second competing product brand

### Requirement: Exported skill entries carry their curated annotation when one exists

The plaza export SHALL carry a skill's curated five-locale annotation into its exported entry when the skills face makes one available upstream, rendering each slot from the annotation's human track rather than zh source text. The zh-in-all-slots fallback applies only to skills with no annotation coverage. The export SHALL NOT read a translation asset the pipeline does not produce.

#### Scenario: An annotated skill renders five distinct locales

- **WHEN** the export runs against a skills face whose entries carry annotations
- **THEN** each annotated skill's exported entry has a distinct summary per locale slot drawn from the annotation, and only un-annotated skills repeat the zh text across slots

#### Scenario: Un-annotated skills stay honest

- **WHEN** a skill has no annotation coverage
- **THEN** its entry carries the zh source text in all five slots with provenance unchanged, never a fabricated translation and never an omission
