## Overall Impression

The four fix passes landed: the failure paths, touch ergonomics, header chrome, and palette are now genuinely clean, and the new drawer is the app's most designed-for scale case. What remains is the second tier: recovery affordances (interrupted answers, unbind), the labeling debt (glyph-only chrome), and one real regression-class bug the refactor exposed. Biggest opportunity: the moments of need — bind codes, quota limits, interrupted answers — have no in-place help or recovery.

## What's Working

1. **Interrupted-turn lifecycle** — socket death finalizes the turn honestly, unlocks the composer, suppresses orphan events; the doc's truncation-never-masquerades rule is implemented, not just written.
2. **The history drawer's scale contract** — 5+15 paging with a counter, groups pinned reachable, REST-first (works with socket down), failed switch toasts and keeps the drawer up. The 100-session case was designed for.
3. **The dead-share page as acquisition** — the one outsider touchpoint refuses to dead-end and hands visitors the demo funnel.
