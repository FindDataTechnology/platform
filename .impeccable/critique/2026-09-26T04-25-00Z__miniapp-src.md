## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: **Authored, with drift at the seams.** "The Daylight Pocket" is legible in the code: paper `#f3f4f6`, white hairline cards, exactly two shadows (both on the outline rail), one Pocket Blue carrying send/bubble/links/running state, the Inverted Ink code surface, the capsule-aware three-zone header chip, the half-screen panel, and WeChat-honest copy (「已生成卡片，点右上角 ⋯ 转发」). No unrelated chat miniprogram could ship this unchanged. The authoring frays at the edges: a default dark native nav bar (#111827) sits on top of the daylight world on every page, off-palette hexes have crept into the stylesheet, emoji block icons import their own colors into the closed palette, and the shipped history page ignores DESIGN.md's own time-grouping/load-more spec.

## Overall Impression

The core loop — open, send, watch a streamed answer with honest interrupted/regenerate states — is genuinely good and survives WeChat's hostile lifecycle better than most apps survive a browser tab. The seams are where it hurts: everything around the loop (chrome, history at scale, error paths, touch ergonomics) reads like a v1 that shipped one pass before its own design doc. The single biggest opportunity: the app currently opens under a dark #111819-style native bar that DESIGN.md's own Inverted Ink Rule forbids, on top of an in-page header — picking one committed header and owning the capsule zone would fix the first impression on every page at once.

## What's Working

1. **State honesty under WeChat's lifecycle.** Backgrounding mid-stream finalizes the turn as interrupted, suppresses the orphaned run's events, and reconnects on foreground (packages/core chat-store.ts L327–355; runtime.ts L154–160). The conn-line is a slim retry affordance, not a panic banner. Hardest problem in the product, solved with care.
2. **WeChat-native manners, correctly learned.** Half-screen SelectionPanel with delayed-visibility close and 75vh cap (app.css L155–265), safe-area insets on every bottom-docked surface, share flow that pre-mints the token and tells the truth about the ⋯ menu, and the never-a-dead-end login skip on two surfaces.
3. **Scroller-padding and keyboard discipline.** Horizontal padding on children exactly per the named rule (.turn-wrap/.share-body), and the composer lifts with the real-px keyboard height — the one place raw px is required.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Systemic undersized touch targets.** At 375pt: send 64rpx=32px, header glyphs ≈26px, chip ✕ ≈11px, outline tab 11×28px, turn actions ≈18px, cron 暂停/删除 ≈12px (app.css L108–113, L704–706, L743–754, L963–974, L1446–1449). Mis-taps on ✕/删除 cause destructive accidents one-handed. Fix: 88rpx minimum hit areas via padding, grow send/hd-btn to 80–88rpx, update DESIGN.md's "generous" bar to 88rpx. *Suggested command: $impeccable adapt*
2. **[P1] One-tap, no-confirm share revocation.** The entire .share-row body triggers handleRevoke instantly (sessions L216–224) — a link already forwarded into group chats dies from a scroll-mis-tap, while cron delete gets a showModal confirm. Fix: restrict to a 撤销 button + the same Taro.showModal confirm. *Suggested command: $impeccable harden*
3. **[P1] Silent action failure when the socket is down; cron errors render inside a closed form.** History rows "open" then navigate back to the old conversation (sessions L141–147); showcase taps no-op; cron_add rejections display only inside a form the submit collapsed (cron L178 vs L330). The history page deliberately works over REST while WS is down, so this state is common and fully unexplained. Fix: gate sends on connected with a toast, or queue+flush on reconnect; keep the cron form open on failure. *Suggested command: $impeccable harden*
4. **[P2] Closed-Palette and One-Pocket breaches.** Red unread dot #ef4444 vs blue session dots; jump flash uses Tailwind blue rgba(59,130,246,.14) instead of a Pocket Blue wash; indigo share banner #eef2ff/#4338ca (sanctioned by a DESIGN.md bug — "indigo" isn't in its own palette); #1f2937 drift. Fix: unify to Pocket Blue steps and correct DESIGN.md. *Suggested command: $impeccable polish*
5. **[P2] The daylight world opens under a dark native nav bar.** app.config.ts keeps the default bar at #111827 with white title on every page — the Inverted Ink Rule's color on general chrome, and chat stacks a second in-page header under it. Fix: either commit to navigationStyle:"custom" and own the capsule zone as designed, or set a light bar per page and drop the duplicate header. *Suggested command: $impeccable polish*
